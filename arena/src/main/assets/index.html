HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap an element, then drag a creature onto your half of the field. It fights on its own.
  • All 50 creatures are ready at all times. No costs, no waiting. Up to 6 on the field.
  • First to 15 KOs wins. Every creature you lose is a point for your rival, so pick your matchups.
  • Elements matter: ▲ green means strong against what the rival has out, ▼ red means weak.
  • Guardians soak damage up front. Snipers and Casters hit from behind them. Dashers dive past and hunt the back line. Brawlers stop the Dashers.
  • A creature heads home after 2 minutes in the arena. Keep at least one out, or the rival scores.
