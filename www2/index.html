How to Play

The goal

Each trainer carries 12 capsules. Every creature you send out uses one. Knock out every creature your rival sends — once they're out of capsules with nobody left standing, you win!

If the 3-minute clock runs out, most KOs wins. Tied? Sudden death: the next KO takes it.

Sending out critters

  • Drag a card onto your (blue) half of the arena — or tap a card, then tap the field. Hold a card to read what it does.
  • Each card costs ☀ sun energy, which refills over time. The last minute runs at 2× energy.
  • Creatures fight on their own: they march, pick targets and use their moves automatically. Your job is what, where and when.
  • Capsules are limited: cheap cards are quick answers, expensive ones get more out of each capsule.

Lead critter

Every battle opens with both trainers sending out a lead at the same time, Pokémon style. You start with 8 energy: a big lead hits hard but leaves less for your follow-up.

Evolution

Critters earn XP by fighting. When one is ready, a gold ⬆ badge appears over it. Tap it and spend a little energy to evolve: +40% HP, a 25% heal, +30% power, a bigger body and a new name. Evolving costs no capsule, so it's often better than sending a fresh critter.

Crowd boost

The crowd gets louder when you score KOs, land super-effective hits and evolve. When your CROWD meter is full, tap it: for 7 seconds your whole team attacks 35% faster and moves 20% faster. Time it for the big clash!

Roles

Types

Each element is super effective (×1.5) against the next one in the circle, and weak (×0.67) against the one before it:

Mystic magic is super effective against Mystic and Dragon. Dragons resist all five elements. Normal is neutral.

Tips

  • Put tanks in front and ranged critters behind them.
  • Splash attacks melt swarms. Swarms melt tanks.
  • Assassins leap over the front line — keep a brawler near your backline.
  • Waiting is a move: let your rival commit, then counter.
  • League trainers further up the ladder draw extra energy from their home crowd.
  • Beat league trainers to win their signature critters for your collection.
